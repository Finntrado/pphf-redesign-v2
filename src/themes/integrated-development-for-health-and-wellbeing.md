## Empowering Communities and Transforming Lives

### How we work across key development sectors for health and wellbeing of people?

PPHF works and linkages across key sectors to impact health and wellbeing of people through essential health, food and nutrition security, education, women empowerment, child protection, skill development, livelihood, agriculture, natural resource management, environmental sustainability reduce poverty, foster economic growth, and protect the environment.

We design gender-responsive actions and ensure a gender lens when considering priorities and programmatic actions across sectors.
