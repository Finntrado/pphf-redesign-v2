## Project Description

To meet the primary healthcare needs of the underprivileged population for NCDs in PHCs and UPHCs.

## Project Objectives

- Build the capacity of health team to deliver essential NCD services.
- Increase public awareness on NCDs through health education.
- Strengthening implementation of population-based screening guidelines at the PHCs.
- Integrate with existing government schemes under ABDM and PMJAY.
