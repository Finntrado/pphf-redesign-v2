## Project Description

PPHF in collaboration with the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)-Centre for Innovation and BioDesign (CIBiOD) incubated start up NLLF (Nirogaya Lifeline Foundation) and with the support of Ericsson CSR, implementing a comprehensive NCD and primary healthcare strengthening program using digital health technology. This initiative aims to complement the government efforts under the Ayushman Bharat Scheme to deliver comprehensive primary healthcare services through PHCs and CHCs.

## Project Objectives

- Unlocking Healthcare Center Potential: Leverage cutting-edge technology to enhance the capabilities of healthcare centers in both urban and rural areas.
- Early Diagnosis and Treatment: Facilitate early diagnosis, treatment, and timely referrals for Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs).
- Improved Accessibility: Make point-of-care diagnostics accessible to all, including those in remote locations.
- Reduced Burden on Tertiary Care: Decrease the pressure on tertiary healthcare centers.
- Strengthening Primary Healthcare: Align with the objectives of PMJAY by strengthening the primary healthcare delivery system.
