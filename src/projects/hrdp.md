## Project Description

The Holistic Rural Development Programme (HRDP) supported by HDFC Bank aims to improve the wellbeing of people in rural areas focusing on contextual and targeted development interventions for empowerment of the communities.  The key interventions are on skill development for livelihood and increase in family income, improving healthcare, increased access to education, and sustainable natural resource management. This multifaceted approach aims to enhance the overall well-being and quality of life in rural areas.

## Project Objectives

- Improve the Socio-economic condition of people.
- Promote income generation activities for women and youth.
- Improve community access to basic health services and WASH.
- Provide safe and clean environment in Anganwadi, Health centers and Primary Schools
