## MY HEALTH: Contributing in improving knowledge and a call for action for adopting appropriate health seeking behaviors

### Program Objectives

- Contribute in improving knowledge and a call for action in adopting the appropriate health seeking behaviors
- Mobilize community for adopting appropriate health seeking behaviors through health and nutrition education
- Use media art and culture for advocating the health messages for community

**Target Beneficiaries:** Pregnant women and mothers having children upto 5 years and adolescent girls
