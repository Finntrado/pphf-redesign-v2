### Key interventions

- Deployment of portable diagnostic devices for early TB detection, ensuring last-mile healthcare access.
- Capacity building for healthcare teams to enhance treatment adherence and patient support.
- Community awareness campaigns to reduce stigma and promote early detection.
- Real-time monitoring via the Nikshay Portal to track progress and improve impact assessment.
