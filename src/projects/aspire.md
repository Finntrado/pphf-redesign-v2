## ASPIRE: Collaboration to Intensify Actions on Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs) Program

### Program Objectives

- Build the capacity of UPHC and Basti Dawakhanas (BDKs) health team to deliver essential NCD services
- Increased public awareness of NCDs through health education and promotion of critical health issues in the community
- Strengthen implementation of NCD population-based screening (PBS) guidelines at the UPHC and BDKs
- Establish linkages between Ayushman Bharat Insurance schemes for improving treatment adherence
