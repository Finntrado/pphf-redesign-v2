The project focuses on enhancing the well-being of Brahmapur Patta saree artisans. Through targeted training in new designs, coloring and dyeing, and weaving techniques, we aim to empower artisans, preserve cultural heritage, and ensure economic sustainability. By equipping them with advanced skills, the project seeks to improve market access, foster community support, and enhance the overall quality of life for the artisans, thereby creating a sustainable and prosperous future.

### Program Objectives

To train 100 local Artisans on weaving, 20 artisans on dyeing and coloring, and 6 artisans on designing of Berhampur handloom patta sarees; and linking them as sustainable livelihood initiative contributing to their overall wellbeing.

Intervention Area Berhampur, Odisha

Training Venue Common Facilitation Centre (CFC), Berhampur
