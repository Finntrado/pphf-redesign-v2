## Project Description

Project Punaraya supported by Sun Pharma aims to strengthen public health facilities by addressing gaps in infrastructure, medical equipment, skilled human resources, and community mobilization. The initiative is designed to enhance the delivery and uptake of essential primary health services under Health and Wellness Centers, thereby reducing out of pocket expenditure and relieving the burden on secondary and tertiary healthcare facilities.

## Project Objectives

- Upgrades health facilities into model health centers with modern infrastructure and improved service readiness.
- Increase awareness and demand for services on MNCHN and NCD.
