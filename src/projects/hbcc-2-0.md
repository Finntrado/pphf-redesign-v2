### No of people impacted 

- More than 1,80,000 people reached through inter-personal communication
- 4 crore reached through mass media
- 2 crore reached through digital media

### Program Objectives

- Increase awareness on prevention, protection and spread of COVID-19 in the community
- Improve hand hygiene knowledge and practices in the community and schools with priority focus on hand washing with soap (HWWS)
- Improve access to personal hygiene materials like soaps to sustain hygiene practices within communities
- Improve capacities of health workers and schoolteachers on hand hygiene practices and COVID-19 prevention
- Advocacy for enhanced commitment and resources for improving hand washing services and coverage
