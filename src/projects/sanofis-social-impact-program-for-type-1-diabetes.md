The project provides best-in-class training and educational tools to (i) diabetologists, (ii) paediatricians, (iii) diabetes educators, (iv) caregivers, and (v) young people with T1D, in order to create standardized care and a network to support people with T1D across India. The program also supports access to treatment for the underprivileged.

### Program Objectives

- Provide access of treatment to underprivileged Type 1 patients to:
- Support disbursal of funds to the Health Care Organizations (HCOs)
- Document disbursal and ensuring utilization of funds
